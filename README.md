# snehalatta

A magical, interactive birthday celebration web application dedicated to **Snehalatta (Bindu & Chinni)** ✨🎂

## 🌟 Highlights
- 🎂 **Smart Microphone Breath Detection**: Accurate microphone-based candle blowing with live sensitivity meter, progressive candle extinguishing, and interactive tap fallback.
- 🐱 **Animated Kawaii Cat Companion**: Reactive companion with wagging tail, blinking expressions, and real-time speech bubbles.
- 📸 **Moments of Grace Photo Showcase**: High-resolution gallery featuring authentic moments with fullscreen lightbox and tap-to-heart reactions.
- 💎 **Essence of Chinni (Virtues & Charms)**: Interactive cards celebrating innocence, calmness, poise, and ambition accompanied by harmonic chime bells.
- 🎈 **Wish Box Balloon-Pop Mini-Game**: 5 interactive floating balloons that pop with confetti showers to reveal personal blessings.
- 🏮 **Celestial Sky Lantern Tribute**: Release floating illuminated lanterns into the open sky with glowing embers.
- 🎵 **Enchanting Birthday Melodies**: Dual-mode audio system with polyphonic celestial music box synthesis and external audio file support.
